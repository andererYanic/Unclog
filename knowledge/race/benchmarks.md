# Benchmarks register (to be sourced)

Status: **empty by design.** Which benchmarks to trust, and how to source and refresh them, is an
open owner decision (brief §20). Numbers in the source video are anecdotal and must not be used
as benchmarks (brief preamble).

Rules for adding a benchmark:
1. Every row needs a source (publisher, title, URL or document), a date, a sample description
   and a region/industry scope.
2. Use per-engagement benchmarks as `StageMetric.benchmark` with `benchmark_source_id`
   pointing to a `Source` record.
3. Refresh or retire rows older than 24 months.
