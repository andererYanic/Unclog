# Industry notes

**Hypotheses, not benchmarks.** These are typical places to *look first*, drawn from TOC practice
and the brief's field notes. They raise priors for the interview, never a verdict: the heat table
and confirmation tests decide. Target segment and first industries are an open owner decision
(§20); extend this when they're chosen.

| Industry | Often the constraint | Look out for | Typical first exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trades / Handwerk (installers, workshops) | skilled crew or workshop capacity; owner's quoting/sign-off time | lead flood vs crew capacity (flooding trap); quotes waiting for the owner | release work by due date; quote thresholds; complete job kits before crew arrives |
| B2B services / consultancies | response speed to inbound leads; senior experts' time | batching of lead follow-up; experts doing admin | continuous lead response; strip admin from experts |
| E-commerce / made-to-order | fulfillment or production capacity; sometimes reach | "where is my order" tickets as a symptom | honest delivery dates; rope on order release |
| SaaS | support capacity, onboarding, or conversion of trials | churn driven by slow support; CEO blaming sales | ticket triage; onboarding checklist; measure trial → paid stages |
